About Me

The short version of a long road.

From the Cab to the Terminal

I spent years operating pneumatic dry-bulk tankers — hauling flour, cement, and other powder products across the country. I ran PTO drying procedures, managed DOT compliance across multiple jurisdictions, and learned firsthand that the hardest problems in trucking aren't on the road. They're in the back office.

That's what pulled me into software. I kept seeing workflows held together by spreadsheets and phone calls — dispatch, invoicing, IFTA reporting — that better tooling could automate entirely. So I started building it myself.

I'm now pursuing associate degrees in AI Engineering and IT, with a goal of landing a salaried AI application engineering role while keeping independent SaaS development going on the side. Everything I build draws on real operational experience that most developers don't have.
