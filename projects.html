SaaS Platform

TruckOps AI

TruckOps AI is a 15-module SaaS platform designed for small trucking carriers running 1–15 trucks. It covers the full back-office workflow: dispatch, invoicing, IFTA fuel tax reporting, load profitability analysis, and DOT compliance tracking.

This project exists because I lived the problem. As a dry-bulk tanker operator, I watched small carriers drown in spreadsheets and disconnected tools. TruckOps AI is the platform I wished existed — built by someone who actually understands the domain.

Key Technical Decisions

  • Anthropic Claude API for intelligent document parsing and compliance alerts
  • Supabase for auth, database, and real-time subscriptions
  • Module-based architecture so carriers can adopt features incrementally

AI Video Production

CrimeCast Studio

CrimeCast Studio is a vertical AI video production platform built specifically for true crime content. It handles the full pipeline: research and script generation, AI voice cloning, image and video generation, automated rendering, and multi-platform publishing.

The production site runs at wrongmancases.com. The entire content pipeline — from source research through final render — is orchestrated through a single Next.js application backed by Supabase.

Production Stack

  • fal.ai (Kling, Seedance, FLUX) for AI image and video generation
  • ElevenLabs for voice cloning and narration
  • Remotion + Lambda for server-side video rendering at scale
  • Cloudflare R2 for asset storage and delivery
